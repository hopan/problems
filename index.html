A catalog of the world's problems, from mosquitoes to AI.

Anyone can submit a problem they're facing, from something small like a mosquito to something huge like climate change, war, or AI. People looking to build something can browse real problems and see who's affected. The system automatically groups similar problems together so duplicates surface as one.
